An automated elevator system in a skyscraper starts at the Ground Floor (Floor 0).

    Every trip upward, it moves up by 4 floors.

    However, due to cable friction, after it reaches or passes Floor 20, its efficiency drops: every trip upward moves it by only 2 floors instead of 4.

    Additionally, every single trip (regardless of floor height) causes 3% wear and tear on the cable's current structural health. The cable starts at 100% health.

How many trips will it take for the elevator to reach or pass Floor 35, OR for the cable health to drop below 60% (whichever happens first)?
